How to play

Every day there's a new song. All the lyrics are hidden as blanks.

  • Type any word you remember. If it's in the song, every place it appears fills in.
  • You don't need to press Enter or worry about capitals or punctuation (dont = don't).
  • Play the 30s clip, or link a YouTube video to hear any line again without seeing its words.
  • Give up when you're stuck. Your score is the share of words you found.

Each line gets a square: 🟩 90%+, 🟨 50%+, 🟥 under half.

Want more? Pick By artist or Any song to play as much as you like.
